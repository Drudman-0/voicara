ABOUT SHE RISES SA

A movement built
around her voice.

She Rises SA is designed as a digital space where women can share experiences, find information and support one another.

OUR PURPOSE

Creating a space where women can feel heard.

Gender-based violence affects people and communities across South Africa. Addressing it requires awareness, support, education and communities willing to stand together.

She Rises SA aims to use technology to create a platform where stories can be shared responsibly and where women can find pathways towards support.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Our values

Compassion

Every person's experience deserves to be treated with dignity and respect.

Safety

Protecting the privacy and safety of people sharing their experiences is essential.

Voice

We believe responsible storytelling can help create awareness and understanding.

Community

Positive change becomes stronger when communities work together.
