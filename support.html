YOU DESERVE SUPPORT

You don't have to
face it alone.

If you or someone you know is experiencing violence, reaching out for help can be an important first step.

Need immediate help?

If you are in immediate danger, contact emergency services or go to a safe place if you can do so safely.

Emergency services 10111 / 112

Need someone to talk to?

Consider reaching out to a trusted adult, family member, friend, counsellor or recognised support organisation.

View resources

Plan for your safety

If you are experiencing violence, consider identifying a safe place and people you trust who you can contact.
