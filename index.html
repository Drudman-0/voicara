SOUTH AFRICA • VOICARA

South Africa stands together.

Every woman deserves to live with dignity, safety and freedom. VOICARA exists to encourage women, amplify their voices and support communities working towards a South Africa free from gender-based violence.

Ekurhuleni / Kempton Park

Investigation continues into deaths of women in Ekurhuleni

September 2026

Authorities are continuing investigations into the deaths of women whose bodies were discovered in the Ekurhuleni region. The developments have raised concern within local communities and have renewed conversations about women's safety and gender-based violence.

News reports have highlighted developments in and around Kempton Park while investigations continue. The exact circumstances surrounding individual cases remain subject to police investigations and official findings.
